XBI RESEARCH AGENDA

八条议程，一条文明主线

Eight tracks, one throughline

从定义生物智能开始，到人类长期未来结束——这是我们认为必须被认真对待的研究顺序。

Starting from defining Biological Intelligence and ending at humanity's long-term future — the sequence we believe deserves serious study.

01

定义生物智能

Define BI

建立生物智能的定义、度量与理论基础。

A working definition, metrics and theoretical foundations for BI.

02

生物智能安全

BI Safety

面向生命系统的安全评估、红队测试与失效模式研究。

Safety evaluation, red-teaming and failure-mode research for living systems.

03

生物智能治理

BI Governance

治理框架、标准与跨机构协作机制。

Governance frameworks, standards and cross-institutional coordination.

04

个人生物智能

Personal BI

个体生命基准、可穿戴感知与长期健康预测。

Personal life baselines, wearable sensing and long-horizon health prediction.

05

AI × BI

AI 与生物智能的交叉方法与双重用途风险管理。

Cross-disciplinary methods and dual-use risk management at the AI–biology interface.

06

BI × Robotics

生命系统与机器人、具身智能的协同设计。

Co-design of living systems with robotics and embodied intelligence.

07

面向太空的生物智能

BI for Space

太空环境下的生命维持、辐射防护与长期任务医学。

Life support, radiation protection and long-duration mission medicine in space.

08

人类未来

Human Futures

面向长期人类文明韧性的情景研究。

Scenario research for the long-term resilience of human civilization.
